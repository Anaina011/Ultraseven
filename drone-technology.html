Drone Technology

In our Drone Technology program, students don’t just fly drones — they learn to build them from scratch, understand the electronics behind flight, and explore how drones are reshaping industries like defense, agriculture, and filmmaking.

1–2 Day Workshop

In our 1 or 2-day workshop, participants will learn the basics of drone technology, including how drones are built and how they fly — a perfect introduction for future drone engineers and pilots

5-Day Bootcamp

In our 5-day bootcamp, students will gain complete hands-on experience by building their own drone and learning to fly it — laying the foundation to become either a drone engineer or a skilled pilot.

Pro Course (Coming Soon)

Our 3-month professional course offers in-depth training in drone design, electronics, flight systems, and real-time projects — and students will build and take home their own custom drone, ready for real-world use
